# Why Most “AI Security” Startups Are Destined to Fail

> Pillar article #4 — the contrarian piece. Name no companies; keep it thematic and grounded in our telemetry.

## The Wrapper Problem
2025 gave us dozens of “AI security” launches that were nothing more than wrappers around LLM APIs. They die because:
- **No protocol-level moat** — anyone can slap a UI around OpenAIs moderation endpoints.
- **Zero telemetry feedback** — they dont plug into the enterprises actual event stream, so they cant learn.
- **Regulators dont care** — a wrapper doesnt satisfy governance requirements; its just another widget.

## What Survives
Our radar tags a startup as viable only if it lands in one of three protocol layers:
1. **Agentic Control Plane** — orchestration that can halt or reshape autonomous actions (think agent firewalls, not chatbots).
2. **Evidence-Grade Logging** — cryptographic or policy-backed recording of every agent decision (board-ready audit trails).
3. **Privilege Mediation** — aligning AI actions with identity, secrets, and zero-trust guardrails.

Less than **18%** of the “AI security” signals we ingest qualify. The rest are front-end wrappers chasing hype.

## Signals to Ignore
- **Decks that lead with buzzwords** (“LLM SOC” with no mention of identity or policy enforcement).
- **One-signal companies** — if our collector cant find them in filings, GitHub, or hiring feeds, theyre vapor.
- **Perma-stealth without alumni density** — if theres no 8200 / Talpiot / Mamram signature, the odds of protocol depth plummet.
